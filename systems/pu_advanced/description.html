The additive system showcased in Toki Pona: The Language of Good is widely referred to as the Pu advanced system, as it is the more complex of the two systems included in Pu. It was the most widespread number system from 2014 to the mid 2020s and is the first standardized number system to gain widespread usage.

In the Pu advanced system, several existing content words are given a numerical meaning. Numbers are created by listing these number words and summing them up. Although words can theoretically be in any order, the examples given in Pu as well as common usage places the larger numbers first. Because of this, deviating from this word order may increase the likihood of misinterpretations.
